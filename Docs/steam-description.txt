[b]Keeps the marriages you chose for your starting colonists from being undone by RimWorld's random breakup interaction.[/b]

Build the colony around the couple you created, without their story ending before it has begun.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Married pawns who begin a new colony together are protected automatically.
[*]The protection lasts for the life of the save.
[*]Protected marriages are marked as a founding bond in the Social tab.
[*]Later recruits and relationships formed after the colony begins are unchanged.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Safe to add to an existing save. Founding marriages are only found when a new game starts, so in an existing save use the Dev Mode action [b]Restore founding marriage[/b] to protect, or restore, one chosen marriage.
