Founding Bonds keeps the marriages you chose for your starting colonists from being undone by RimWorld's random breakup interaction.

[b]Features[/b]
[list]
[*]Automatically protects married pawns who begin a new colony together.
[*]Keeps that protection for the life of the save.
[*]Marks protected marriages as a founding bond in the Social tab.
[*]Leaves later recruits and relationships formed after the colony begins unchanged.
[/list]

Build the colony around the couple you created, without their story ending before it has begun.
